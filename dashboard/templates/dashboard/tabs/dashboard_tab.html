The limit is the smaller, secondary figure because the request is what is actually reserved -- but it is present, because "we requested 4,000 cores" and "we may burst to 8,000" are different facts. Neither is cluster capacity. The estate has no capacity figure, so nothing here is drawn as a fraction of one. {% endcomment %}
